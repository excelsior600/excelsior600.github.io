About

I’m Excelsior, also known as VexServin. I work on the people side of Roblox games: finding the right creators, managing the ones who make content, and looking after the community that grows around a game.

Whether a game needs a bigger creator roster, a steadier content pipeline or a community that stays active and welcoming, I cover all three.

Roles

CCC

Content Creator Collector

Finds creators who fit a game, builds and organizes the roster, and keeps track of who is making what.

CCM

Content Creator Manager

Runs the creator program day to day: onboarding, updates and briefs, and regular check-ins so creators have what they need.

CM

Community Manager

Looks after the community spaces around a game: moderation, announcements, events, and turning player feedback into something the team can act on.
